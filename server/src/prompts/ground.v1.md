TASK: ground
For each numbered claim, decide whether the source supports it. "supported" = the source states it, or it is a faithful
restatement (same subject, same facts, numbers and dates; relative dates resolved against the source date are fine).
"unsupported" = it adds anything the source doesn't say, changes a number, date or person, turns a question,
hypothetical or plan being considered into fact, or presents what the assistant or another person said as the user's own fact.
Each claim comes with the quote the extractor gave; the quote must actually back the claim.
